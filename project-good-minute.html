Project overview

Good Minute is a restaurant located in Barisal, Bangladesh. As a co-owner of the restaurant, I felt the need for a reliable way to track profitability, sales and costs from month to month.

Business problem

Financial and sales records were available but analysing them manually made it difficult to quickly understand the restaurant's performance. The key business questions were:

  • Is the restaurant profitable?
  • Which menu items and categories generate the most sales?
  • Where are costs concentrated and how are these changing over time?
My contribution

I independently developed and maintain the complete analytics solution from data preparation and modelling through to dashboard design and monthly reporting.

Approach
  • Structured monthly sales and expense data in SQL by item, category and cost type.
  • Developed DAX measures for Profit, Profit Margin %, ROI % and other performance indicators.
  • Designed three Power BI pages: Executive Overview, Sales Analysis and Cost Analysis.
  • The dashboard is updated and reviewed monthly to analyse changes in business performance.
Tools & methods

SQLPower BIDAX

Key findings

The analysis highlights which menu categories and items contribute most to sales, how revenue and expenses change over time and which cost categories have the greatest influence on profitability.

Business relevance

These insights support monthly pricing, menu, cost-control and staffing decisions. They provide the ownership team with a consistent, evidence-based view of business performance rather than relying on manual records or intuition

Limitations

Actual financial figures are commercially sensitive and jointly owned with a business partner, so they are not published in this portfolio. The interactive demonstration therefore uses illustrative data while preserving the structure, KPIs and analytical functionality of the original dashboard.
