Good Minute — Restaurant Financial & Sales Dashboard

Independent professional project · Ongoing since 2024 Power BIBusiness Intelligence
Built and independently maintain a 3-page Power BI dashboard, updated every month since 2024, that the business actively uses to track performance.
Project overview

Good Minute is a real restaurant I co-manage. It needed a reliable way to track profitability, sales, and costs month to month, without hiring outside help. I built that system myself and still run it every month.

Business problem / research question

How can a small, owner-operated restaurant get ongoing, reliable visibility into its financial performance without dedicated finance staff?

Project classification

Independent professional project — real, ongoing business, not an academic exercise.

My contribution

Sole builder and maintainer. I designed the SQL data model, wrote the DAX measures, and built every page of the dashboard independently.

Approach
  • Built a SQL database to track monthly revenue (by item and category) and expenses (materials, salary, utilities)
  • Modelled key measures: Profit, Profit Margin %, and ROI %
  • Designed three dashboard pages: an Executive Overview (KPI cards and trends), Sales Analysis (top items, trends), and Cost Analysis (cost breakdowns)
  • Query and refresh the data monthly to keep the business's view of performance current
Tools & methods

SQL, Power BI, DAX

Key findings

The dashboard is used operationally each month to track profit margin trends, identify top- and bottom-performing menu items, and monitor cost categories against revenue.

Business relevance

Gives a small, owner-operated business the kind of ongoing financial visibility that's normally only available with dedicated finance staff.

Limitations

Real financial figures are commercially sensitive and co-owned with a business partner, so specific numbers aren't published here.
