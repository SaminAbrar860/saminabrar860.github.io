About

From the bridge of a ship to the dashboard.

I spent a decade as a commissioned officer in the Bangladesh Navy, progressing from Officer Cadet to Lieutenant Commander. I held roles across intelligence, communications, supply, and executive command. In each of these roles, I was consistently responsible for turning raw information into decisions people could act on — personnel records, resource allocations, navigation logs, and operational data.

After retiring in 2024, I built on that foundation with formal technical training. I'm now completing a Master of Business Analytics at Adelaide University, where I developed my skills in Python, R, SQL, SPSS, Power BI, and Tableau. Alongside my studies, I co-own and manage a small restaurant, Good Minute. There, I independently built and still maintain the SQL database and Power BI dashboard that tracks its financial performance every month. This isn't a classroom exercise — it's a real business I'm accountable for.

My academic work spans econometrics, HR analytics, predictive modelling, applied machine learning, and business intelligence. I'm less interested in mastering one narrow toolset. I'd rather be useful wherever a business has a decision to make and data to make it with.

Predicting Food Deserts in the United States
Group academic project (5 members) · Managing with Big Data
Machine Learning Big Data

Which socioeconomic and geographic factors best predict whether a U.S. community becomes a "food desert" — an area with poor access to affordable, healthy food?

Random Forest achieved the best balance of precision and recall (F1 = 0.896), with income, vehicle access, and food-assistance participation as the strongest predictors.

Scripted vs. GenAI Chatbot — South Australia Tourism Assistant
Group academic project (5 members) · Chatbots & Text Analytics
Applied AI Text Analytics

Does a generative-AI chatbot actually outperform a traditional rule-based one for real users seeking tourism information — or is the simpler option still better?

Predicting M&A Acquisition Targets
Individual academic project · Predictive & Visual Analytics
Finance Machine Learning

Written from the perspective of a hedge-fund analyst: which financial characteristics make a U.S. firm more likely to become a merger-and-acquisition target?

Built a logistic regression model on real firm-level data (2012–2022) showing that profitability, cash reserves, debt, and R&D spending — not firm size or location — are the reliable signals.

Does Working From Home Improve Productivity?
Individual academic case study · People Analytics
HR Analytics Statistical Modelling

Among 90 remote call-centre employees, what actually drives motivation and productivity — autonomy, organisational support, or scheduling flexibility?

Work autonomy was the strongest driver of both motivation and productivity, and its effect on productivity runs partly through increased motivation. Flexibility, on its own, made no measurable difference.
